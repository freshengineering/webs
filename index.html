About

From council jobs to Tier 1 projects

Fresh Engineering is a traffic engineering and road safety consultancy in Melbourne. Our traffic engineers have worked on everything from small council jobs to Victoria’s largest road and rail projects. We know what gets built and what road authorities will approve, and we keep our advice clear and practical.

The Department of Transport and Planning (DTP) has accredited us for traffic management design and prequalified us for road safety auditing.

We have accredited auditors on the Victorian Road Safety Auditors Register, including a Senior Road Safety Auditor who leads our audits.

Areas we serve
