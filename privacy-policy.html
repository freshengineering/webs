Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how Fresh Engineering Pty Ltd (ABN 63 673 624 863) collects, uses and protects your personal information. As a small business we may not be covered by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), but we choose to follow the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Information we collect

We only collect personal information you choose to give us, such as when you send an enquiry. This can include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Your company or organisation name.
  • The service you need and details about your project or enquiry.

When you visit our website, we also collect general usage information through Google Analytics. This includes the pages you view, your device and browser, your approximate location (city or region) and how you found the site. We look at this in total and don’t use it to identify you.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Reply to your enquiry and send quotes.
  • Deliver our engineering and consulting services.
  • Keep you updated on your project.
  • Understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it.

We don’t use your details for marketing lists, and we don’t sell them.

3. Who we share it with

We don’t sell, trade or rent your personal information. We only share it when the law requires us to, or with service providers who help us run our website and business. These are:

  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • Formspree, which processes enquiries sent through our contact form and emails them to us.
  • Google, which provides Google Analytics.

Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States.

4. Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics uses cookies to tell visits apart and measure how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The website still works if you do.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and project details only for as long as we need them for the reason you gave them to us, or as long as the law requires. After that we delete them.

6. Keeping it secure

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure.

7. Access, corrections and complaints

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it. You can also tell us if you have a concern about how we’ve handled your information. Contact us using the details below and we’ll respond within 30 days. If you’re not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
