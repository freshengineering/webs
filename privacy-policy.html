Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Fresh Engineering Pty Ltd (ABN 63 673 624 863) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including but not limited to:

  • Name and contact details (email, phone number).
  • Company or organisation name.
  • Information regarding your project or enquiry.

When you visit our website, we also collect general usage information through Google Analytics, such as the pages you view, the type of device and browser you use, your approximate location (city or region) and how you found the site. We use this in aggregate and do not use it to identify you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the purpose of:

  • Responding to your enquiries and providing quotes.
  • Delivering our engineering and consultancy services.
  • Communicating with you regarding project updates.
  • Understanding how visitors use our website so we can improve it.

3. Disclosure of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose information only if required by law or to trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our website and business, provided they agree to keep this information confidential. These include:

  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • Formspree, which processes enquiries sent through our contact form and delivers them to us by email.
  • Google, which provides Google Analytics.

Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States.

4. Cookies and Analytics

Google Analytics uses cookies to tell visits apart and measure how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The website will still work if you do.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights and Complaints

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you and request corrections. Please contact us if you wish to exercise these rights or have a concern about how we have handled your information. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
