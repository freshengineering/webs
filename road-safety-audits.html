What is a road safety audit?

A road safety audit (RSA) is an independent review of a road design, an existing road or roadworks by accredited road safety auditors. It finds where someone could be killed or seriously injured, and recommends ways to reduce that risk.

It is not the same as a compliance audit. A compliance audit checks that what's on the road matches the approved plan or permit. A road safety audit looks for crash risk that remains, even when everything matches.

We work for councils, the Department of Transport and Planning (DTP), developers and contractors across Melbourne and regional Victoria. We also hold Traffic Management Designer (TMD) Category 1, 2 & 3. As a team, we have 15+ years on Victorian projects. Our project experience includes North East Link, the West Gate Tunnel and Level Crossing Removal Package 2. We know how a road actually gets built.

Road safety audit stages we cover

We follow the Austroads Guide to Road Safety Part 6: Road Safety Audit, which sets out when audits happen so risk is caught while it's cheapest to fix.

  1. Feasibility Route and layout options
  2. Preliminary design Geometry, sight distance, pedestrians and cyclists
  3. Detailed design Signs, line marking, barriers, lighting
  4. Pre-opening Day and night site inspection before the road opens
  5. Post-opening Site inspection once the road is in use

Outside the design stages, we also audit:

  • Existing roads: site inspection and crash history review
  • Roadworks and temporary traffic management: audits of traffic management setups on live roads

Who needs a road safety audit

Audits are asked for by the people who own, approve or build the road.

  • Councils: intersection upgrades, new roads, subdivision works and changes to existing roads
  • DTP projects: audits at the stages the project requires
  • Developers: where a planning permit condition calls for an audit
  • Major (Tier 1) and civil contractors: roadworks and temporary traffic management audits on live roads

Not sure whether your project needs one, or at which stage? Read our guide: When do you need a road safety audit in Victoria?

Road safety audits of roadworks and temporary traffic management

Works on live roads change how people drive, walk and ride through a site. A roadworks audit reviews the traffic management plan (TMP), the traffic guidance scheme (TGS, the sign and cone layout drawings) and the setup on the road. Principal contractors and road authorities commonly ask for one on DTP roads and larger projects.

We also design traffic management, so we know how a plan is drawn and how it works on the road. A roadworks road safety audit is not the same as a traffic management audit, which checks that the setup matches the approved TGS. Send us the TMP or TGS for a quote.

A Safe System approach

Findings are framed around the Safe System. People make mistakes, and the human body can only tolerate so much crash force. So we focus on the risks most likely to cause fatal and serious injury, and recommend treatments that make a mistake survivable.

Accredited road safety auditors in Victoria

In Victoria, DTP accredits road safety auditors. Austroads manages the Victorian Road Safety Auditors Register for DTP, so you can check any auditor. We have accredited auditors on the register, including a Senior Road Safety Auditor who leads every audit. Fresh Engineering is also prequalified by DTP for road safety auditing.

The audit team is independent of the design team, as Austroads and DTP require. That keeps the findings objective and credible when an authority reviews them.

  • Audit team independent of the designers
  • Accredited auditors on the Victorian register, led by a Senior Road Safety Auditor
  • Prequalified by DTP for road safety auditing
  • Assessed against Austroads Part 6 and DTP requirements

How a road safety audit runs

  1. Scope and quote You send the drawings and scope. We confirm the stage and quote a fixed fee and timing.
  2. Drawing review The audit team reviews the design package, or the TMP and TGS for roadworks.
  3. Site inspection We inspect the site. A pre-opening audit includes a day and a night inspection.
  4. Audit report Risk-rated findings by location, with practical recommendations.
  5. Response and close-out The client and designer respond to each finding in the response table. A close-out meeting is optional.

What's in our road safety audit report

A report a designer can act on. Each finding is located, the road users at risk are named, and the risk is rated so you know what to fix first.

  • Risk-rated findings by location
  • Practical, buildable recommendations
  • Annotated plans and site photos
  • Response table to close out each finding
  • Optional close-out meeting

Frequently asked questions

Does every project need a road safety audit?

Not every project. Audits are routine on DTP projects and on Big Build projects (Victoria's major road and rail program). Councils often ask for one through a planning permit condition or their own policy. Your permit or contract will say which stages you need. For the detail, see when a road safety audit is required.

Who can do a road safety audit in Victoria?

Austroads Part 6 says an audit is done by a team that is independent of the design. In Victoria, DTP accredits road safety auditors, and road authorities usually ask for accredited auditors on the team. Check your permit or contract for what it requires. Our audits are done by accredited road safety auditors who had no part in the design, led by a Senior Road Safety Auditor.

How do I check a road safety auditor in Victoria is accredited?

Search the Victorian Road Safety Auditors Register. Austroads manages the register for DTP, and anyone can search it. Our auditors are on the register.

What's the difference between a Road Safety Auditor and a Senior Road Safety Auditor?

They are the two levels of auditor accreditation in Victoria, and DTP sets the rules for both. A Senior Road Safety Auditor has more audit experience. To reach Senior level, an auditor must first complete formal audits under a Senior Road Safety Auditor's guidance, including audits at design stages. Our audits are led by a Senior Road Safety Auditor.

How is a road safety audit different from a compliance audit or a Safe System assessment?

A compliance audit checks the road against the approved plan or permit. A road safety audit looks for the crash risk that is left, even when everything matches. A Safe System assessment is a separate exercise that looks at how well a design aligns with Safe System principles. Our guide explains how the three compare.

How much does a road safety audit cost?

There's no flat rate, because the price depends on the stage, the size of the design package and the site inspections needed. Send us the drawings and scope for a fixed-fee quote, or read what a road safety audit costs.

How long does a road safety audit take?

It depends on the stage, the size of the project and how complete the drawings are. Design-stage audits are mostly a review of the drawings. A pre-opening audit needs day and night inspections, so book it early around your opening date. We'll give you the timing with the quote.

What happens after the audit report is issued?

The client and designer respond to each finding in the response table, either accepting it and nominating a treatment or declining it with reasons. The auditors don't redesign the job, so independence holds through close-out. We're available for a close-out meeting to talk the findings through. If you're responding to another auditor's findings, see our advice on responding to road safety audit findings.

Do you work outside Melbourne?

Yes. We audit across regional Victoria, and our project experience includes South Australia. Talk to us about interstate work.
