Tender Support & Project Delivery

The bid lives or dies on the traffic methodology. We help you stage the works and price the occupancies properly at tender, so you're costing what you can actually build, not a guess.

At tender

We work alongside your estimators to build the staging logic and put real numbers behind it. The methodology drives plant, program and occupancy cost, so it's worth nailing before the price locks in.

  • Staging strategy and traffic management concept
  • Realistic durations, quantities and occupancy costs
  • Aligned to DTP or council requirements and contract conditions
  • Constraints and risk flagged early
  • A defensible methodology your bid can stand on

After award

Once you win, we turn the concept into accredited Traffic Management Designs and get them authorised. On the declared arterial network that means the Road Occupancy and MoA process; on local roads, the responsible council's conditions of consent.

  • Accredited TMDs and staging plans (Cat 1, 2 & 3)
  • Road safety audits at the relevant stage
  • MoA, permits and road occupancy applications
  • Authority and council liaison
  • Documentation to AGTTM and DTP supplements

Through construction

Staging shifts on site, so we stay involved. Design revisions, query responses and on-site support keep the works moving and the approvals current.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I bring you in?

Before the price is locked. The staging methodology shapes plant, program and occupancy cost, so the cheapest savings are found early. We can still help later, but you lose that leverage.

Do I need an accredited TMD?

Yes. Temporary traffic management designs on the Victorian road network must be prepared by an accredited TMD at the right category. We hold Categories 1, 2 and 3, from local roads to major arterials.

What drives the cost?

The road environment, number of stages, occupancy complexity and how much audit and authority liaison is involved. We scope and quote against your actual job, not a flat rate.

Can you prepare the traffic management section of a tender submission?

Yes. We build the staging methodology and traffic management concept, put durations and quantities behind it that your estimators can price, and write it up so the assessors can see the job has been thought through. It's the part of the bid a road authority reviewer reads most closely.

Do you work for subcontractors as well as head contractors?

Yes. We support traffic management subcontractors and civil subbies pricing packages under a head contract, as well as the Tier 1s themselves. The accreditation requirements are the same either way, and so is the approach: a methodology you can actually build at the price you've put in.
