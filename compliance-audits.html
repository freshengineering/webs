Traffic management compliance audits

A compliance audit is an independent on-site check that the traffic management actually set up on the ground matches the approved TGS or TMP, the permit conditions and AGTTM. We're a separate set of eyes confirming what's in the field reflects what was signed off.

We audit across Melbourne metro and regional Victoria, day or night. With TMD Cat 1, 2 & 3 accreditation, Senior Road Safety Auditor accreditation and 15+ years on jobs from council works to North East Link and the Level Crossing Removal Program, we check the setup the way the road authority will.

Compliance audits at a glance

  • Independent check against approved TGS or TMP
  • Day and night setups, under live traffic
  • Findings ranked critical to observation
  • Dated photos tied to clause or plan
  • Critical issues flagged on site
  • TMD accreditation, Cat 1, 2 & 3

What we check on site

We attend the site and work through the setup against the approved documents and AGTTM under live conditions. Where the works run after dark, we audit the night setup too, because lighting, delineation and sign legibility behave differently.

  • Setup matches the approved TGS or TMP
  • Signs, tapers, buffers and delineation against AGTTM
  • MoA covers any temporary speed limit
  • DTP road occupation licence or council permit conditions met
  • Day and night setups, under live traffic

What you get

You receive a written report listing each finding ranked by risk, every non-conformance tied to a clause or plan detail with dated photos, and a clear fix for each one. Anything critical, we tell you on site so it gets corrected straight away.

  • Report ranking findings critical to observation
  • Photos and measurements for each item
  • Each non-conformance tied to a clause or plan
  • On-site call-out for critical issues
  • Re-inspection and close-out if needed

Who it's for

Contractors use audits to show due diligence and meet client and principal-contractor requirements. Councils and asset owners use them to check permitted works on their roads, and we run ongoing audit programs across larger packages.

  • Tier 1 and civil contractors
  • Councils and asset owners checking permitted works
  • Developers working on public roads
  • Lane and road closures, detours, intersections
  • One-off inspections or project-wide programs

Frequently asked questions

How is a compliance audit different from a road safety audit?

A road safety audit looks for where crash risk remains in a road or design; a compliance audit asks whether the setup on the ground matches what was approved. We do both.

When do we need one?

There's no blanket rule, but they're increasingly required by permit conditions, client specs and principal-contractor procedures, especially on DTP roads and after a near-miss.

How fast is the report?

Usually a few business days. Critical, life-safety issues we flag on site immediately rather than waiting for the report.

How much does a compliance audit cost?

It depends on the number of sites, whether we're inspecting day and night setups, and whether it's a one-off inspection or an ongoing program across a package of works. Travel to regional sites is factored in too. Give us the site details and we'll quote it as a fixed fee.

How often should roadworks be audited?

There's no statutory frequency. In practice: at initial establishment, after each significant change to the layout or stage, and periodically on long-running sites, with night checks where the works run after dark. Permit conditions or the principal contractor's procedures sometimes set a minimum, so we align the program with those.
